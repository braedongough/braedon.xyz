# Core Palette — 16-Bit Sega Genesis Inspired

A highly saturated, arcade-era palette inspired by early-90s Sega Genesis pixel art.

## Color Philosophy

- Use a **limited, highly saturated palette**
- Favor **hard color boundaries** over smooth gradients
- Avoid modern muted, desaturated, or gray-heavy colors
- Use **deep navy instead of pure black** for most outlines
- Create contrast through **warm oranges/yellows vs. cool blues/cyans**
- Bright colors should feel **neon and arcade-like**, not soft or pastel
- Keep the overall appearance reminiscent of a **16-bit game displayed on a CRT**
